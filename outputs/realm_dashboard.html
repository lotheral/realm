01 — Overview

What is REALM?

REALM is a swarm-intelligence simulation engine. It builds a population of synthetic agents from three parallel signals — birth-chart astrology, Big-Five personality scores, and country-level Hofstede culture — and runs them through a tick-based social platform where they post, engage, drift, and react to transiting celestial events. The aim is a working laboratory for testing how personality, culture, and timing compose into collective behaviour.

03 — Validation

What is the scientific basis?

Two validation studies back the engine. Big-Five facet output is checked against the public Johnson IPIP-NEO-120 dataset (N=612,711). Astrological output is checked against 22 hand-authored celebrity profiles with expert-rated expected traits.
